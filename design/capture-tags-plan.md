# Optional tags on a capture

**Shipped; deployed August 6, 2026**, and folded into Dunlin rather than promoted
to a release of its own. Tags reused `lists.Tag` rather than a parallel model,
and stayed optional at capture time by design: writing something down must never
force a categorisation decision.

That rule outlived the model it was written for. `Capture` is deleted; the
knowledge core's typed tags follow the same principle, and a tag typed at capture
now becomes a confirmed concept.
