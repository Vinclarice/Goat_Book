## What v4 refuses

- **Commerce, permanently** — billing, pricing, packaging, entitlements, a wedge,
  a market. Not deferred.
- **Analytics.** Its only job was telling whether a wedge landed, and there is no
  wedge. `/privacy/`'s absolute claim is test-held and stays true for free.
- **Deciding V1 by default.** Building only the deepening branch is a decision
  against guests; building the invitation is a decision for them. **The spine is
  what lets the question stay open honestly**, and that is the whole design of
  this file.
- **Row-level security and connection pooling.** §6's triggers are *sharing or
  paying tenants* and *an observed connection ceiling*. Commerce being refused
  makes the first one further away, not closer.
- **A staging environment as a precondition.**
  `security-and-resilience-plan.md` establishes nothing in it waits on staging.
  Do not let this plan acquire that gate by inheritance.
- **An asynchronous queue for import.** §6 puts the queue behind staging; an
  import that takes a minute inside a request is ugly and honest. If it genuinely
  needs a worker, that is a trigger firing and §6 should be told, not worked
  around.
- **v3's refusals, all of them** — the graph as the primary surface, generated
  narrative as the primary voice, an event bus, a `Bill` primitive, auto-pinning
  a drafted day, automatic carry-forward, closing a day retroactively, inbound
  calendar sync.

## Risks with no answer yet

1. **An open question with no deadline becomes a closed one without anybody
   deciding.** V1's cost of delay is stated above precisely because this is the
   likely failure, and it is the failure this corpus is otherwise very good at
   catching.
2. **Importing Vince's own archive answers the corpus gates in a way a guest's
   material would not.** His archive shares his biases, which is the exact
   criticism `product-stories.md` makes of its own stories. **Firing a gate is
   not the same as validating what it gates**, and a search increment that ships
   because the corpus got big enough is still tuned by one person's judgment.
3. **Import is the first act that writes thousands of rows at once**, into a
   product with no pagination, no queue and a synchronous proposal pass on the
   live capture path. Three absences individually fine and jointly the shape of
   an outage.
4. **The documents will lag again.** Four stale items in one session, all in
   plans written within the last three days. Nothing here fixes the cause.
