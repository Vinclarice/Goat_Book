Mockup — fake data, nothing is saved. Ticking, picking and the composer all work; use the three moments to see the day change shape.
What this replaces: both the Day page and the Agenda, and the Areas they draw from. One page, one date. The left column is the day: appointments, the list you chose for it, a line, and the log of what happened. The right column is the pool — every open line you have, which is what the Agenda becomes once there are no Areas to file into. Nothing on this page is a copy of anything else: the list is DailyFocus, the log is a read over records that already carry a time, and the pool is every active Item the owner has.
The four rules it draws: the morning's list is written for a day and never on it; the first act of execution — a tick, a Did or a Today, never a Note — draws the line under it; a tick is a log line with a time; anything that joins later sits below the line and is counted apart from what was chosen. The pool prunes itself — a line unpicked for three weeks asks one question and then leaves.
