# Planning assistant — shipped

An evidence-backed proposal inbox: commitments read out of writing, connections
between notes, unresolved questions, project briefs, the weekly review's loose
ends and a drafted week — every proposal showing its evidence and requiring
confirmation, with no generation anywhere.

Six increments, designed and built August 18–19, 2026. Shipped and deployed
August 19 as `kestrel`. The narrative, its four decisions and what they taught
are in [`roadmap-history.md`](roadmap-history.md).
