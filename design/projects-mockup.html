Projects

Signature element: a thin composition bar under each title, segmented by that project's own areas using the same color a person already knows from the side nav and area pages. Segment width follows each area's open-task share, so "Launch the business" (three areas, uneven load) reads differently at a glance than "Website Relaunch" (two areas, close to even) — without opening either one. A project with no areas yet gets an empty dashed track, read as an invitation rather than a broken chart.

New: creating a project used to live only in the Agenda sidebar, a step removed from the page actually about projects — title (and now, optionally, a due date) in, land straight on the new project's own page. Two more additions below: both the name and due date become editable on a project's own page (today there's no way to set either after creation), and a due date in the past now reads as overdue the same way an overdue task or area already does.

Each project's colored strip shows how its open work is split across areas.

New: the composition bar has no built-in explanation otherwise — the per-segment name and count only show up on hover (or, on the workspace page, in the area list right below it), which a touch device can't do at all. One line, once, above the whole grid rather than repeated on every card.

Project workspace

Same page structure as today's shipped ProjectRoute — header, Areas list, add-area forms, danger zone — with the wider composition bar dropped directly under the meta line, so the project's own shape is the first thing under its name, not a separate chart section someone has to scroll to. Everything below the bar (forms, buttons, danger zone) is unchanged behaviour.

New: the title and due date are both editable now (same "disabled until it actually differs" pattern AreaRoute already uses for its own rename field — Save name below is greyed out until the text changes), and a due date in the past gets the same overdue treatment as the card grid. Two more additions below, both guidance copy: a caption under this page's own composition bar, and a quiet hint on an empty due date so a blank field reads as "none set" rather than "forgot to fill this in."
