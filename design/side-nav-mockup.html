This puts one left nav on all three, and resolves the three-column problem the plan flagged by moving filtering out of the sidebar and into the agenda header as chips — leaving the sidebar to do only what its name says.

1. Wide — ≥1400px, three columns

Left nav navigates, header chips filter, right rail keeps the things that are neither: new list, tag cloud, daily digest toggle. This is the only width where all three fit.

3. Narrow — below ~760px, disclosure

What this decides

Sidebar navigates, header filters. Today the agenda's sidebar does both — clicking a list there filters rather than navigates. Splitting them is what makes one nav usable on all three pages.
The right rail is the thing that yields at medium width, not the left nav, so navigation is the one constant.
Inbox joins the nav, which is also how Capture stops being reachable only by typing the URL.
Open counts live in the nav, so they're visible from the list page and archive too, not just the agenda.
